Been and Atlasi both answer "how many countries have I been to?" They do it in opposite ways.

**Been** is a map you tap. A country lights up, your passport stats move, and you can export a card. **Atlasi** is a passport grid of 227 countries and territories, then a log of the specific places inside each one. If you want the wider field first, see the [best apps to track countries visited](/blog/best-apps-to-track-countries-visited). If you already know you are leaving Been, the [Been alternatives roundup](/blog/best-been-app-alternatives) covers six options.

_Atlasi is our app. We've tried to be straight about what it does and doesn't do — the head-to-head table below includes where it loses._

## The Short Version

*   **Atlasi** is the one to download. You stamp 227 countries and territories, log the places inside them, import trips from geotagged photos, and send a friend a list that opens in a browser. It is iOS-only today and has no 3D globe.
*   **Been** wins only if you want the fastest country count, a shareable map image, offline use, and iOS plus Android. Region tracking and the 3D globe are Premium.

Choose Atlasi unless you specifically need a tap-to-fill percentage and nothing else.

## Deep Dive: Been

Been (been.app), from REBR AS, has 1M+ downloads on iPhone and Android. You tap a country and it fills in. The free passport shows countries, continents, UN members, and recent trips.

### Where Been Shines
*   **Speed**: You can mark a lifetime of travel in a few minutes.
*   **Offline map**: The world map is bundled in the app, so a plane cabin still works.
*   **Cross-platform sync**: Apple or Google sign-in keeps iPhone and Android in sync.
*   **Regions (Premium)**: 1,000+ states, provinces, prefectures, and counties across 50+ countries. England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland can count separately while the UK still counts once toward UN members.
*   **Shareable passport image**: One tap to a branded map card.

### Where Been Falls Short
*   **Places**: Free Been is a country (and, with Premium, region) checklist. It is not a restaurant or hotel log.
*   **Photos**: There is no camera-roll import that rebuilds trips from geotags.
*   **3D globe is paid**: The globe screenshot that sells the app is a Premium feature.
*   **Notes are paid**: Private notes per country require Premium. Free Been will not hold the "why this country counts" story.

Been uses de-facto borders and labels disputed territories rather than picking a political side. Your percentage is a share of the list Been lets you collect, so two apps will rarely show the same number.

## Deep Dive: Atlasi

Atlasi is built for the gap Been leaves: the specific spots inside a country.

You still stamp countries — 227, including territories such as the Faroe Islands and French Polynesia. Then you log entries as Food, Stay, Place, or Experience. Google Places fills in the address. You can publish a list as a public link. Photo Import scans your camera roll, clusters nearby geotagged photos, and suggests places to confirm.

### Where Atlasi Shines
*   **Actionable data**: A friend can follow the list, not guess from a green Italy.
*   **Photo Import**: Past trips come from photos you already took, not from memory.
*   **Privacy default**: Trips stay private until you publish a list or trip.
*   **Low battery impact**: Location is read when you log, not in the background.

### Where Atlasi Falls Short
*   **No 3D globe** and no state/province layer.
*   **Active logging**: Miss a tap and the place is missing. Been's country tap is faster for a pure tally.
*   **iOS only**: Android is in development. Been already ships on both.
*   **Subscription, not a one-time buy**: Unlimited entries and photo-import trips are $9.99 per month or $49.99 per year. Been Premium is a single purchase.

## Pricing

**Been free**: mark 250+ countries, passport stats, share the map, offline, sync. **Been Premium**: one-time purchase for regions, 3D globe, unlimited wishlist (free cap is 5), private notes, no ads. Check the store listing for the current price; Been does not put a dollar figure on been.app.

**Atlasi free**: all 227 countries, unlimited trips, 10 entries per trip, 5 social saves per month, one photo-import trip. **Atlasi Premium**: $9.99 per month or $49.99 per year, 7-day trial — unlimited entries, photo-import trips, and social saves.

If you never log places, Been's free tier is the cheaper way to keep a count. If you do log places, Atlasi's free cap (10 entries per trip) is the limit that matters, not the country map.

## The Verdict

**Choose Atlasi if:**
*   You want the country count and the restaurants, stays, and experiences inside it.
*   You want your camera roll to rebuild the trips you forgot to log.
*   You would rather share a list than a colored map.

**Choose Been if:**
*   You want a percentage of the world in under five minutes and nothing else.
*   You need Android, offline, or a 3D globe.
*   State and province tracking is the next layer you will actually use.

**Use both if:** Been holds the tally and Atlasi holds the places. That split is cleaner than forcing one app to do the other's job.
