So why did I build Atlasi?

Because I love to travel, but there was no app that fit my needs. Screenshots of Instagram recos buried in my camera roll. Restaurant names I swore I'd remember but didn't. Photos from incredible trips sitting in folders I never opened.

I'd been to some of the most beautiful places on earth, and I had nothing to show for it. No record. No way to share it. Just scattered memories across a dozen apps.

So I built something that actually keeps up with how we travel now. Atlasi captures places from the content you're already scrolling. It can even scan your photo library and piece together trips you took years ago. And it makes everything look good enough to share. And it looks a lot better than the other country tracking apps, if I do say so myself.

I think you'll feel the difference the first time you use it.

And if you have any feedback, I'd love to hear it. Just drop me a reply.
