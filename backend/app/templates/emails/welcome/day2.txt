So why did I build Atlasi?

Honestly? I'm a designer by trade, and every travel app I tried made me cringe. Visited, Been, all of them. They felt clunky and looked worse. I've been to some of the most beautiful places on earth, and I was tracking them in apps that looked like they were designed in 2012.

I wanted something that felt as magical as traveling itself. Something that was powerful but dead simple. Something I'd actually want to share.

So I built it.

Atlasi is designed to be beautiful first, because your travels deserve that. But it's also built to be insanely shareable. Your passport view, your map, your stats. All of it looks good enough to post.

Give it a spin. I think you'll feel the difference.
